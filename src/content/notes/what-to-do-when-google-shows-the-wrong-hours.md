It's 5:40 on a Tuesday. You locked up at 5. A neighbor pulls into your lot because Google said you're open until 6. They try the door, sit in the truck a minute, and drive to the place down the road.

You never hear about it. Wrong hours don't send you an angry email. They just quietly send people somewhere else.

The good news is that this is one of the easiest things on your listing to fix, and one of the easiest to keep fixed.

## Why is Google showing the wrong hours?

Usually it's one of a few plain reasons.

- **The hours were set once and never touched again.** You changed your schedule two summers ago, and the listing didn't hear about it.
- **Someone suggested a change.** Customers can suggest edits to your listing, and Google sometimes accepts them. A well-meaning neighbor who stopped by on a slow day can end up changing your hours.
- **Google filled in the blanks.** If your listing is thin, Google may pull hours from your website, an old directory, or what it sees from visitors.
- **Holidays were never added.** Your regular hours are right, but Google still shows you open on Thanksgiving.

None of this means you did anything wrong. It just means nobody has looked in a while.

## How do I fix my hours on Google?

If you've claimed your listing, sign in with the Google account that manages it. Search your business name on Google or open it in Google Maps, and you should see the option to edit your profile. Your hours are in there.

A few things to check while you're in there:

- Every day of the week, including the days you're closed.
- Lunch breaks, if you close for one. You can set more than one time block in a day.
- Any changes Google or a customer has suggested. Accept the right ones and turn down the wrong ones.

If you haven't claimed your listing yet, that's the first step. Until you do, anyone's suggestion carries about as much weight as yours.

## What about holidays and special hours?

Google has a separate spot for special hours. Use it for holidays, inventory days, a family event, or the week you're closed for vacation. Special hours show up for just those dates, and your regular hours come back on their own afterward.

It takes a minute, and it saves someone a wasted drive on the one day they finally made time to come see you.

## How do I keep my hours right after that?

Pick one small habit and stick to it.

- **Check once a month.** Look yourself up the way a neighbor would. Make sure the hours still match the sign on your door.
- **Update before you change, not after.** If summer hours start June 1, change the listing the week before.
- **Add holidays at the start of each season.** Do them all at once, a few months out.
- **Don't forget the other places.** Your website, Apple Maps, Yelp, and Facebook all show hours too. When one changes, change them all the same day.

The goal is simple. Wherever a neighbor looks, they get the same answer, and it's the true one.

## One thing to check this week

Search your own business on your phone, the way a customer would. Look at today's hours. Then look at the door.

If they match, you're in good shape. If they don't, you just found the easiest fix on your whole listing, and the next neighbor who pulls into your lot will find you open.
