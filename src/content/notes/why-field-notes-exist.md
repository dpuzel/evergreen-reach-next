Field Notes is where we write down the useful stuff. The kind of thing we'd tell you over coffee if we were sitting across from you at the shop.

## What belongs here

We're not building a magazine. We're not chasing keywords. These are short, honest notes about:

- Google listings that look forgotten
- Websites that quietly rot
- How a small business actually gets found by the right people nearby

> If a note helps you see the work more clearly, it did its job. If you want someone to handle that work with you, that's what the rest of this site is for.

The first three notes are on this shelf now. More will land when we have something useful to say.
